📖The Story of the 2nd Edition

The 1st Edition belonged to Zackary & Lalaina — and this year The Palace split them as rivals, Zackary with Tamu, Lalaina with Daniel. Neither could repeat it.

Instead the crown went to Luca & Kenny. Luca was mathematical — cold, precise, every cup accounted for. Kenny was the underdog of the tournament who chose the biggest stage to erupt, lighting two On Fires in the Final itself. They left with the $100 prize and Wantanga FC Champion Shirts, presented by President Sayor.

And the On Fire throne, vacant since Paolo's 1st-Edition reign, was seized by Leonie — the hottest at The Palace, who kept the flame burning even as her team fell in the Final.
