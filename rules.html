Rules

3rd Edition — 6 cups · 3-2-1 formation
New for the 3rd Edition: knockout ties are now settled by a Shootout (see §3). Carried over from the 2nd Edition: 6 cups · 3-2-1 "Christmas Tree" formation · 1 re-rack per team per match · time limits · On Fire as the centrepiece.

1. Objective

Eliminate all opposing cups before yours are gone. Common sense, fair play, tolerance for chaos.

2. Setup

6 plastic cups per team, arranged in a 3-2-1 "Christmas Tree" formation — 3 at the back, 2 in the middle, 1 at the front.

Cups hold water only (hygiene, less waste, fewer splashes). Beer from separate personal cups.

3. Time Limits

Tables in useMatch length
2 tables20 minutes
1 table12 minutes

When time runs out:

  • Pool match → result is a draw at the current cup count.
  • Knockout match → the team with the most cups remaining wins. If the cups are level, it goes to a Shootout (below).

🎯 Knockout Shootout — only when the cups are level at time:

  • Teams take alternating single shots — one shot per player, turn about.
  • If both teams miss in a round, each team adds a cup and the duel continues.
  • One redemption only: if a team hits, the other gets a single redemption shot to stay alive — and it must be taken by the player next in the shooting order. Miss the redemption and the match is lost.

4. Line Violations

Called by the defending team before or at the moment of the shot. No retrospective appeals. No VAR. No Hawk-Eye.

Glasses and contacts wearers are the most entitled to rule on close calls.

5. Defensive Rules

  • Direct shot in flight: do not interfere.
  • Bounce shot (table then cup): you may block it after it hits the table.
  • Ball contacting a cup: you may block once contact is made.
  • Ball circling the rim: you may blow it away before it touches water.

6. Scoring

  • Standard shot: 1 cup eliminated.
  • Bounce (table → cup): the target cup plus 1 defender-selected cup. Remains blockable — defenders may interfere after it hits the table.
  • Double (both teammates score the same cup in the same round): the target cup plus 2 defender-selected cups. The ball is removed after the first shot.
  • Called shot (declare a specific cup before shooting): if hit → the declared cup plus 1 bonus. If wrong cup hit → only that cup, no bonus. Maximum 2 called shots per player per match.
  • Island (declare on a visibly isolated cup before shooting): that cup plus 2 defender-selected cups. Once per player per match.
Combination principle: compatible mechanics may stack. Overly creative interpretations may be vetoed by consensus.

7. ⭐ On Fire

Scoring on 2 consecutive turns = you are On Fire.

Declare it — and it counts toward your On Fire leaderboard total.

Score again on the 3rd consecutive turn = retain possession and keep shooting until you miss.

8. Accidental Own-Cup Elimination

Knock your own cups over and they count as eliminated. Gravity is unforgiving.

9. Re-Racks

1 re-rack per team per match. Must be requested before a throw begins.

10. Optional Pre-Match Rules (by mutual agreement)

  • Sudden Death: intentionally hitting an opponent's held beer cup = instant win. Not valid in the Final.
  • Catch Penalty: a direct shot that fully overshoots the table may be cleanly caught; if caught, the shooting team removes one of their own cups.

11. Disputes & Final Provision

Disputes resolved by majority consensus, sporting logic, and whichever argument is delivered most convincingly.

The Organising Committee reserves the right to interpret ambiguity and occasionally pretend to know exactly what it's doing.
